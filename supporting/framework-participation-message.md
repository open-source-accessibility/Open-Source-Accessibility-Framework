## Accessibility

This project participates in the [Open Source Accessibility Framework](https://github.com/open-source-accessibility/Open-Source-Accessibility-Framework).

We are using the framework to make accessibility improvements visible, trackable, and ongoing. Our accessibility goals, planned actions, progress, and remaining barriers are documented in our [public framework tracking issue](TRACKING_ISSUE_URL).

This participation reflects a commitment to continuous improvement. It is not a certification or a claim that the project is fully accessible. We welcome feedback and accessibility bug reports from users and contributors. Please see our [accessibility documentation](ACCESSIBILITY_DOC_URL) for supported environments, known limitations, workarounds, and reporting instructions.
