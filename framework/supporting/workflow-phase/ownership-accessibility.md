### Responsibilities

The accessibility owner is responsible for:

- Triaging accessibility reports and assigning an owner, severity, status, and target resolution date.
- Tracking accessibility work, known barriers, and upstream blockers.
- Coordinating accessibility reviews for significant user-facing changes.
- Keeping the project's accessibility documentation, contributor expectations, and issue-reporting process current.
- Sharing status updates and escalating unresolved accessibility risks to project maintainers.

### Handoff process

When accessibility ownership changes:

1. The outgoing owner documents open reports, current priorities, known blockers, temporary workarounds, and outstanding commitments in **[tracking location]**.
2. Project maintainers identify the incoming owner and update `ACCESSIBILITY.md` and any other ownership references, such as `README.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `CODEOWNERS`, or the project tracker.
3. The incoming owner reviews the handoff information, confirms access to the required project tools and communication channels, and acknowledges responsibility.

If the accessibility owner is unavailable, **[fallback maintainer, team, or role]** assumes these responsibilities until a new owner is assigned.
